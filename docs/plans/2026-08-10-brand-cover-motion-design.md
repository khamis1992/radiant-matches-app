# Brand cover motion

## Goal

Give photo-less artist cards a premium sense of motion without replacing or redrawing the original GLAM asset.

## Motion

- Reveal the cover once from the brushstroke direction when it enters the page.
- Continue with a very slow, uniform drift and scale to add depth without distracting from artist details.
- Use no additional sparkles, colors, gradients, or decorative brand elements.
- Disable all movement when the operating system requests reduced motion.
