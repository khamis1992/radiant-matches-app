# Shops Living Window Hero

## Direction

Replace the chapter-based Shops hero with a single continuous editorial scene. The hero behaves like a living shop window: an original vertical GLAM beauty film fills the upper frame, while a compact solid Ink editorial strip below provides one clear message and one action.

## Visual hierarchy

- Original Higgsfield video: a restrained Doha boutique vanity featuring unlabeled skincare, fragrance, lipstick, compact and brush.
- Original GLAM logo and back control sit over the deliberately quiet upper video area.
- One small contextual label identifies the weekly edit without competing with the imagery.
- The lower Ink strip contains a short directory proposition and a single Rose Action circular CTA.
- Filters and search remain outside the hero on the light Porcelain shopping surface.

## Motion and accessibility

- Video is muted, inline, looping and lazy enough to preserve initial page responsiveness.
- Playback pauses when the hero leaves the viewport.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` pauses the video while preserving the complete static composition.
- Controls meet the 44px minimum target and have visible keyboard focus.

## Brand compliance

- Component colors use shared GLAM Tailwind tokens only.
- The page remains light-first; Ink is limited to the premium hero strip.
- No decorative stars, sparkles, crowns, gold or invented gradients.
- The original logo asset is used unchanged.
- Pink is limited to the label and primary action.
