# Shops immersive story hero

## Direction

Turn the shops header into a three-chapter product story while preserving the
existing video asset and GLAM's light-first visual system.

## Chapters

1. Trusted, curated beauty picks.
2. Independent local shops.
3. Seamless delivery to the customer.

## Interaction

- The active chapter follows video playback time.
- Three progress controls communicate position and allow direct seeking.
- Video pauses for `prefers-reduced-motion`; chapters remain manually usable.
- Back and logo controls respect the safe area and 44px touch targets.

## Brand rules

- Product video remains color accurate with no pink filter.
- Copy uses Inter / IBM Plex Sans Arabic, not a decorative serif or script font.
- The only dark surface is the premium hero caption panel.
- All component colors use GLAM design tokens.
